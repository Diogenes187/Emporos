Welcome aboard, spacer

Four steps from here to the stars:
⚓
A world. Pick a campaign up top — or hit New Campaign to open a fresh ledger.
👤
A life. Open the Crew tab and roll a Traveller — the full lifepath, term by term, every roll audited by the rules engine.
🌌
The map. Import a sector, then click any world for its survey dossier — starport, air, law, distance. Set Course drafts your jump order and ◈ Jump runs it: navigation, fuel, and the dice.
🗣
The referee. Type plain words in the box at the bottom: “I look for cargo work at the docks.” Mechanics stay outside narration — travel, trade and combat change only when the engine commits them.
