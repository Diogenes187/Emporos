Assault

An unshielded mind that suffers an assault is rendered unconscious immediately. The victim suffers 2D6 + Effect damage, applied to psionic strength (if the victim has it), then Intelligence, then Endurance. Intelligence returns at the rate of one point per day.

When a shielded mind is assaulted, the attacker and defender make opposed Telepathy checks. Assault takes 1-6 seconds, is Formidable (-6), and costs 8+range psionic strength points.
